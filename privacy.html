Privacy Policy

At preDoctr.pk, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when using our platform and LMS services.

Data Security & Sharing

We implement robust industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions, secure cloud storage with restricted access controls, and periodic security audits.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared with trusted service providers strictly as required to process payments or deliver platform features.

We use essential cookies for user authentication, session security, and basic platform functionality. Anonymous analytics cookies may be used to help us understand feature usage and optimize user experience.
